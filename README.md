# Hotel Booking Data Visualization Using R

## Week 2 – Data Visualization and Insight Communication

This project presents a detailed visual analysis of the **Hotel Booking Demand Dataset** using **R and ggplot2**. It is a continuation of the Week 1 data cleaning and exploratory data analysis project.

The cleaned dataset contains **87,221 hotel booking records** and **33 variables**. The objective of this project is to communicate important booking patterns, trends, distributions, and relationships through clear and informative visualizations.

## Objectives

- Create informative visualizations using R.
- Analyze hotel booking and cancellation patterns.
- Identify monthly and seasonal booking trends.
- Compare Average Daily Rate (ADR) across hotel types.
- Analyze the relationship between lead time and ADR.
- Study the contribution of different market segments.
- Communicate insights clearly to a non-technical audience.

## Key Insights

- City Hotel has more bookings than Resort Hotel.
- Approximately 27.5% of the cleaned bookings were cancelled.
- City Hotel has a higher cancellation rate than Resort Hotel.
- July and August show particularly high booking demand, with August recording the highest overall booking volume.
- Booking lead time has a strongly right-skewed distribution.
- Lead time and ADR show almost no meaningful linear relationship.
- City Hotel has a higher average ADR than Resort Hotel.
- Online Travel Agents (Online TA) represent the dominant market segment.
- Both hotel types demonstrate clear seasonal booking patterns.
